What LootPick is, and what it is not

LootPick pulls together game information, store prices, discounts, price history and review scores, and turns them into a Buy Now Score so you can decide when to buy. It also holds your wishlist, your backlog, your logged purchases, alerts you set, and a feed of current free game giveaways.

LootPick is not a store. We do not sell games, process game purchases, hold keys or licences, or take part in anything you buy elsewhere. When you follow a link out of LootPick you are dealing with that store, or with a giveaway run by someone else, under their terms. Any problem with an order, a key, a refund or a download is between you and them.

LootPick is independent. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any game publisher, developer, storefront or platform holder.

Prices, discounts and scores are supplied by third parties and refreshed on a schedule. They can be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Always confirm the price on the store page before you pay.

Prices and currency

Store prices in LootPick generally come from sources that publish in US dollars, for a US or global storefront. When you set a display currency other than the source currency, we convert using a fixed indicative rate held in the app. That converted figure is an approximation for comparison only. It is not a quote, it is not a live exchange rate, and it is not what your store will charge you. Regional pricing, local taxes, payment fees and your own card conversion can all move the real number.

The Buy Now Score

The Buy Now Score is our own calculation, built from things like discount depth, critic and player scores, how close a price sits to its historic low, and where a game ranks in your own list. It is an opinion expressed as a number. It is not advice, not a valuation, not a prediction that a price will or will not fall further, and not a promise that a deal is good. Where the app offers different scoring presets, they weight the same inputs differently and none of them is a guarantee. What you buy is your call.
